Why Twyne

Less swiping. More meaning.

Most apps reward endless scrolling. Twyne is designed around one thing: helping two people actually get to know each other.

One match at a time

Give someone your full attention. With a single active match, there’s no juggling a dozen chats, just room for a real connection.

Likes that say something

Every like comes with a written message about the part of their profile that caught your eye. No more silent taps.

Real, verified people

Selfie verification and photo review help keep Twyne full of genuine people who are there for the same reason you are.

How it works

From hello to “we should meet.”

Build your profile

Add your best photos, answer a few prompts, and pick an accent color that feels like you.

Get verified

A quick selfie check confirms you’re you, so everyone can feel comfortable.

Send a thoughtful like

Browse, find someone who stands out, and tell them why in your own words.

Start talking

When it’s mutual, you’re matched. Focus on one conversation and see where it goes.
