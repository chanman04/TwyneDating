Common help topics

I can’t sign in or my account is locked

Email us from the address on your account and tell us what you see. If your account was suspended, we’ll explain why and what, if anything, you can do next.

Billing and membership

Memberships are billed and managed by the App Store or Google Play. To cancel, open your Apple ID or Google Play subscription settings. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their own policies. For anything else, email us.

Report or block someone

Use the report and block options on a member’s profile or in your conversation. If you need to report something urgent, email us with details. In an emergency, contact local emergency services first.

Report a bug or give feedback

Tell us what you were doing, what you expected and what happened, plus your device and app version. Screenshots help a lot.

Privacy requests (access, correction, copy of your data)

Email us from your account address with what you’d like. See our Privacy Policy for your rights and how we handle data.

Delete your account

Option 1: delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Twyne and go to Account.
  2. Tap Delete account, then confirm with Yes, delete my account.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. It removes your profile, photos, messages, matches, likes and verification data from Twyne, and we ask our service providers (identity verification and subscription management) to delete their records for you. Photos and voice notes may linger in network caches for a short time, and backups made before you deleted age out on a rolling schedule. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription, so cancel that in your store account settings too.

Option 2: request deletion here

If you can’t open the app, or no longer have it installed, send a request and we’ll delete your account. To protect you, we confirm the request by replying to the email address on the account before deleting. We aim to finish within 30 days of confirming.

This opens your email app with a pre-filled message to hello@oddpearstudio.com. Press send there to submit. Prefer to write it yourself? Email hello@oddpearstudio.com with the subject “Twyne account deletion request”.
