The basics

What is Twyne?

Twyne is a dating app for people looking for a long-term relationship. Instead of endless swiping, it’s built around thoughtful likes, one active match at a time, and real conversations.

When can I download it?

Twyne is coming soon to the Apple App Store and Google Play. A web version is also on the way. Want to try it before everyone else? Sign up to be a tester.

Where is Twyne available?

We’re launching city by city, starting with the New Orleans and Baton Rouge areas in Louisiana. If you’re outside an open area when you sign up, you’ll join a waitlist for your city and we’ll let you know as soon as it opens.

Who is Twyne for?

Adults who are ready for something meaningful. Twyne is open to everyone: anyone can like anyone, and for same-sex pairings, either person can make the first move.

How matching works

What does “one active match at a time” mean?

You can only have one active match at once. It keeps your attention on a single person and a single conversation, rather than a dozen chats you can’t keep up with. If it doesn’t work out, you can end the match and move on.

How do likes work?

Every like includes a written message, and it’s tied to the part of the profile you liked, such as a photo or a prompt answer. That gives the other person real context and a reason to say yes.

Can I see who liked me?

Yes. Your likes are organized into Liked (people you’ve liked) and Liked You (people who’ve liked you), so you always know where things stand.

Can I take back a like?

You can delete a pending like you’ve sent, but you can’t edit it, so make it count.

Can I personalize my profile?

Yes. Build your profile from curated modules like photos and prompts, and pick an accent color that suits you. We keep it tasteful, so every profile stays clean and easy to read.

Messaging & membership

Is Twyne free?

Creating a profile and browsing is free. Messaging with your match is unlocked with a monthly membership. Pricing and the details of what’s included are shown in the app before you subscribe, and you can cancel any time through your App Store or Google Play account.

How do I cancel my membership?

Memberships are managed through the App Store or Google Play, depending on your device. Open your account’s subscription settings and cancel there.

Safety & privacy

How do you keep fake profiles out?

New members complete a quick selfie verification, and profile photos are screened before they appear. Members can also report and block anyone who makes them uncomfortable, and our team reviews reports.

Who can see my profile?

Only verified, approved members in your area can discover your profile. You can block someone at any time.

Can I delete my account and data?

Yes. You can delete your account from within the app, and doing so removes your profile and personal data from Twyne.

Who makes Twyne?

Twyne is made by Odd Pear Studio.

Testing

How can I become a tester?

Fill out the short form on our tester sign-up page. We’ll email you with instructions to install the test version on iPhone or Android.

I found a bug. What do I do?

Testers get a feedback channel with their invite. Please include what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. Screenshots help a lot.
